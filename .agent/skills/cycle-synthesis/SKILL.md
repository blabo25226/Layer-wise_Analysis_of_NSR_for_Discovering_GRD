# Cycle Synthesis

Every ~3 completed cycles or after a high-priority result, summarize:
- supported findings
- negative findings
- invalidated claims
- repeated failure modes
- unresolved uncertainties
- hypotheses to abandon
- top next directions
- compute spent vs knowledge gained
- items for human review
