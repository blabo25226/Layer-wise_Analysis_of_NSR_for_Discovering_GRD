# Replication Gate

Replication is required or strongly preferred for results that are:
- unusually large
- scientifically surprising
- mechanism-level
- contrary to prior evidence/literature
- dependent on one seed/split/family
- reviewer-identified as fragile

Where feasible change at least one:
seed, split, equation family, noise/corruption, initial condition, or clean rerun environment.
