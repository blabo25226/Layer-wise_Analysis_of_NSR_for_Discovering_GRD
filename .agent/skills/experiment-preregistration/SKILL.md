# Experiment Preregistration

Freeze:
- primary hypothesis
- primary endpoint
- secondary endpoints
- statistical unit
- train/validation/test split
- sealed final test
- baseline conditions
- ablations
- seeds
- checkpoint/model
- hyperparameter search space
- candidate/decode/search budget
- training budget
- operator constraints
- failure/exclusion policy
- significance/equivalence policy
- Go/No-Go
- supported criterion
- unsupported criterion
- undecidable criterion
- compute ceiling
- artifact contract

State explicitly:
**What cannot change after final-test access?**

Require methodology, statistical, and reproducibility review before the full experiment.
