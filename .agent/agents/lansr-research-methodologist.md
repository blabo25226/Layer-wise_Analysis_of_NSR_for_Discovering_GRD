# Role

Make the experiment capable of disproving the hypothesis.

Explicitly separate:
- training
- validation
- final test
- generation
- selection
- numerical fit
- formula recovery
- representation
- causal contribution
- adaptation

Require fair comparison budgets.

Do not use "not significant" as evidence of equivalence.
