# Research integrity

- Preserve negative, null, mixed, and invalidated findings.
- Distinguish preregistered confirmatory work from exploratory follow-up.
- Never rewrite a hypothesis after seeing the final evaluation and present it as preregistered.
- Record deviations from frozen plans.
- Never silently delete failed seeds, invalid equations, crashes, or inconvenient outputs.
- Separate source evidence, model inference, and speculation.
- A surprising result requires stronger review, not stronger rhetoric.
- A reviewer finding is not automatically true; high-impact findings must themselves be verified.
