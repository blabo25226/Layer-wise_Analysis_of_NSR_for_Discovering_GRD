# Human Intervention Policy

Default: continue autonomously.

Do not ask the human to choose between ordinary scientific alternatives if evidence and a safe experiment can resolve them.

Hard stops:
- wrong branch
- destructive change
- private/confidential data concern
- credential/access change
- unavoidable test leakage
- major compute ceiling increase
- unsafe hardware/storage
- research ethics/licensing issue

Interesting results go to `human_review_queue.md` without blocking the loop.
