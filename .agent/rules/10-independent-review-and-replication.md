# Independent review and replication

A cycle conclusion must not rely only on the implementer or primary analyst.

Independent review should inspect primary artifacts:
- frozen plan
- code/config and relevant diff
- raw outputs
- analysis
- statistics
- exclusions/failures
- claims and novelty evidence

Suggested reviewer verdicts:
- ACCEPT_AS_NEGATIVE
- ACCEPT_AS_PRELIMINARY
- REPLICATE
- REVISE_ANALYSIS
- INVALIDATE

A CRITICAL issue blocks promotion of the claim until resolved.
Large, surprising, fragile, mechanism-level, or literature-contrary results require a replication gate.
