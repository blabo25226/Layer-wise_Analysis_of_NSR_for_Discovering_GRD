# Symbolic regression scientific contract

Never equate numerical fit with symbolic recovery.

When applicable, keep separate:
- trajectory / prediction fit
- exact expression recovery
- skeleton / structural equivalence
- symbolic/algebraic equivalence
- variable/regulator recovery
- equation complexity
- validity/failure rate
- singularity and domain safety
- in-distribution vs out-of-distribution generalization
- compute/search budget

Store generated equations and failure reasons at problem level. Do not report only aggregates.
