# Human intervention and review queue

Human review is sparse and should not block routine research.

Do not ask the human to choose between ordinary scientific alternatives when a safe experiment can distinguish them.

Put notable items in `GPU_RUNmultiAI/human_review_queue.md`:
- `none`: routine
- `normal`: useful to inspect
- `high`: surprising, strategically important, fragile, or potentially publishable

High priority alone does not stop the research loop.

When human input is truly required, state:
- what is blocked
- why autonomous resolution is unsafe/impossible
- the smallest decision needed
- what can continue independently
