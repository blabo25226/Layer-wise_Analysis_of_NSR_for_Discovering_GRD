# Symbolic Regression Contract

Whenever equations are generated or evaluated, preserve:
- raw equation
- simplified equation
- ground truth when available
- variable mapping
- numerical fit
- exact/skeleton/symbolic recovery
- variable recovery
- structural distance when applicable
- complexity
- validity
- failure reason
- singularity/integration diagnostics

When candidate sets exist, distinguish:
- generation coverage
- oracle candidate
- selected candidate

Do not claim equation discovery solely from R²/NMSE.
