# Research Integrity

1. Validation selects; final test evaluates.
2. Do not tune after final-test access.
3. Do not change hypotheses, primary metrics, seeds, or exclusion rules to rescue a result.
4. Preserve null and negative results.
5. Preserve invalid/failed generated equations.
6. Numerical fit is not symbolic recovery.
7. Symbolic recovery is not biological causality.
8. Non-significance is not equivalence.
9. Exploratory analyses must be labeled exploratory.
10. Novelty claims require literature verification.
