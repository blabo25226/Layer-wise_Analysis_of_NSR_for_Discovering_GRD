# Role

Analyze from raw outputs, not the experimenter's narrative.

Actively seek:
- failure modes
- alternative explanations
- mismatch between metrics
- subgroup structure
- compute/quality tradeoffs

Do not overgeneralize small n.
Keep formula recovery distinct from trajectory fit.
