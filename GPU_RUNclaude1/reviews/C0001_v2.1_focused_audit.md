## 6. MAJOR findings

### V21-MAJ-1 — PC4's frozen eligible-set count is not what the frozen verifier produces, because the verifier has a 17% false-negative rate

**The claim.** §7.7.1's PC4 row and §0.3's Q12 state the eligible set as *"instances where `R_i`
differs textually from `T_i` **and** the rewrite verifies function-preserving (measured: **170/170**)"*.

**Measured, using exactly the verifier §7.7.0 rule 1 freezes** (`numeric_equivalent(n_points=32,
seed=0)` on the parsed trees):

```
PC4 eligible: 141/170        ineligible: 29
ineligible by family: {'R01': 9, 'R05': 20}      all 29 in stratum H
of those 29, GENUINELY function-preserving under exact rational arithmetic: 29/29
```

**All 29 rejections are false negatives.** Under `sympy.nsimplify(..., rational=True)` followed by
`simplify`, every one of the 29 is exactly the same function. Two mechanisms produce the false
negatives, and both are float non-cancellation: `parse_infix_component` quantizes numerics to
**4 significant digits** (`src/gpu_run4/ted.py:11 NUMERIC_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS = 4`), so
`0.1954 * 0.8392 = 0.16397968` becomes `0.164` — a relative error of ~1.2e-5 against
`numeric_equivalent`'s `rtol = 1e-5`; and sympy `Float` arithmetic on the un-parsed strings likewise
fails to cancel to exact zero. I confirmed both routes independently give 141.

```
orig: 0.1954 + 0.8878 * x_0 * 1/(0.8392 + x_0) + -1 * 0.3968 * x_0
rew : (-0.3968*x_0*(x_0 + 0.8392) + 1.0832*x_0 + 0.16397968)/(x_0 + 0.8392)
rew after parse: (((0.164 + (1.083 * x_0)) + ((-0.3968 * (0.8392 + x_0)) * x_0)) * (1 / (0.8392 + x_0)))
```

**Second-order consequence.** The two families v2.1 reports as contributing **no** gain are R01 and
R05 — **exactly** the two families whose instances the verifier rejected. So "6 of 8 families" is
itself partly a verifier artifact; under exact arithmetic it would be 7 or 8 of 8.

**Consequence for the run: none, and the error is conservative.** Every gating threshold passes with
large margin on the realized eligible set — gain 100 ≥ 40 (2.5×), gain_H 100 ≥ 20 (5×), families
6 ≥ 3 (2×), eligible 141 ≥ the frozen minimum of 40 (3.5×). Exclusions come out of the *denominator*,
and the 29 excluded instances are all in H where the gains live, so PC4's demonstrated capability is
**understated**, not overstated. And no gate is stated as an equality on the eligible count: Gate
A→B (iii) is the three thresholds only, §10.3's `undecidable` list has no PC4 eligible-count clause,
and §14 item 10 covers only the ≥ 40 minimum. **PC4 passes either way.**

**Why it is still MAJOR.** A frozen, disclosed measurement (Q12) will be contradicted by the run's
own `partA_controls.json`, and §14 item 9 forbids re-deriving an eligible set after the fact, so
there is no clean path to reconcile it at run time. Under R1 and rule 01 item 9 a disclosed value
the run will contradict has to be right before the freeze.

**Required fix** (text + one line of code): add an exact-arithmetic fallback to §7.7.0 rule 1 —
`sympy.nsimplify(orig − rewritten, rational=True)` compared to 0 — before an instance may be labeled
`ineligible_rewrite_unverified`, and restate Q12/§7.7.1 as `eligible 170/170 under the corrected
verifier` (or, if the verifier is left as is, as `141/170 under the frozen verifier, 170/170 under
exact rational arithmetic, 29 false negatives enumerated in R01 and R05`).

### V21-MAJ-2 — the same verifier is **anti-conservative** on the negative controls, and §7.7.0 does not say so

§7.7.0 rule 2 applies the same machinery with the opposite verdict: for PC3a/PC3b an alteration is
eligible iff `numeric_equivalent` finds it **not** equivalent. A false negative there — the exact
error class V21-MAJ-1 measures at 17% on PC4 — admits a **genuine no-op** into the eligible set,
where M3 correctly matches it and it is scored a **specificity failure**. Enough of those pushes
PC3a/PC3b below 95% and yields `undecidable (matcher specificity insufficient)` in the gain branch.

So the verifier's error direction is **conservative for positive controls and anti-conservative for
negative ones**, and §7.7.0 presents rules 1 and 2 as symmetric applications of one mechanism without
noting this.

**Does not materialize on this corpus.** Measured PC3b 60/60 = 100% and PC3a 48/48 = 100%, i.e. 5%
headroom (3 systems for PC3b, 2 instances for PC3a) and zero current failures. And the 10 R08 no-ops
were correctly *excluded*, so the mechanism is currently working in the right direction there.

**Required fix.** Record the asymmetry in §7.7.0, and apply the same exact-arithmetic fallback to
rule 2 — an alteration may be declared function-changing only if exact arithmetic also says so.
